### `// ABOUT.ME`

```diff
+ ECE student who thinks a breadboard is a love language.
+ I break things on purpose, then panic when they stay broken.
```

I'm an **Electronics & Communication Engineering** student who spends equal
amounts of time soldering circuits and questioning why the circuit — and
frankly the universe — refuses to work as intended. Currently deep in
**embedded systems, hardware prototyping, and drone swarm concepts**,
because apparently one drone was never going to be enough chaos for me.

I don't just *use* technology — I like to open it up, stare into its many
eyes like the seraphim above, and ask *"how do you actually work"* until it
either confesses or catches fire. Usually both.

```
> current.arc      : building things nobody asked for, on time nobody gave me
> weapon of choice : hackathons, breadboards, 2am "just one more fix"
> known bugs        : me
```

---

### `// THE MAKER'S CURSE`

I build from scratch because buying it finished feels like cheating on a
boss fight. Hackathons are my natural habitat — sleep-deprived, over-
caffeinated, holding a prototype together with hope and electrical tape.
Half my "projects" started as a 3am idea I refused to let die.

- ⚙️ Prototyping electronics until they either work or become modern art
- 🧠 Engineering solutions that are 10% genius, 90% duct tape
- 🕯️ Hackathon survivor — sanity not guaranteed, results occasionally are

---

### `// TECH ∩ MIND`

Here's the part that makes me weird at parties: I'm as obsessed with **why
a circuit behaves the way it does** as I am with **why a brain does**.
Electronics and psychology are basically the same puzzle wearing different
outfits — inputs, feedback loops, things quietly overheating under
pressure. I study one to build machines, and the other to understand why
I keep debugging code at 3am instead of sleeping like a normal signal
processor.

> *Ask me about PCB traces and neural pathways in the same sentence.
> I will not apologize.*

---

### `// SIGNAL & AESTHETIC`

Turns out I don't only build things that beep — I also build things that
*look* good doing it. I edit video, cut short-form content, and dabble in
digital marketing, because a good idea deserves a good cut and a decent
thumbnail. Consider it visual storytelling for the terminally online.

- 🎬 Video editing & short-form content
- 📈 Digital marketing (yes, I know the algorithm is also just a circuit)
- 👁️ An eye for making dark, moody things look intentional
